THE IDEA, WITHOUT THE JARGON

From “something is wrong”
to “here’s what to check next.”

Think of Coldpath as a careful operations assistant. It helps organize a decision; it does not replace the person responsible for it.

01

Notice the warning.

Read a shipment’s temperature trend and compare it with the delivery rules. An early clue can matter before an ordinary alarm fires.

See the recorded warning ↗
02

Make the options clear.

Compare possible responses and their assumptions. If the evidence changes after approval, stop and ask for a fresh review.

Follow the decision ↗
03

Check what happened.

A response is not the same as a recovery. Keep the observations, the outcome and the limits—even when the result is disappointing.
