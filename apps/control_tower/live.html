    Environment & data controls

    A deterministic first-order thermal model generates readings from the seed, cooling state and injected fault. It is an educational browser model, separate from the Python benchmark simulator and trained risk model. Review is triggered by observed threshold breaches or a rising temperature trend. Cold-storage action pulls temperature toward its setpoint; recovery needs five consecutive in-range, stabilizing samples. Sensor-drift scenarios require inspection and cannot be auto-verified. Proposals expire after five new samples or a fault change. Pause freezes progression; hidden tabs suspend without catching up. Reloading starts a new run. Export a run before reset to retain it. Historical evidence stays in the separate evidence workspace.
