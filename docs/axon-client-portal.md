# Axon Services operations portal

The client-facing product replaces the personal portfolio home, introduction overlay and case-study navigation. Root and live.html present the same Axon Services application: overview, shipment register, exceptions, activity, shift handover and environment settings. These are operational views of one stateful workspace. The latest user wording supplies the Axon Services name; no assertion of daily customer adoption, fleet integration, production hosting or business outcomes is added.

Design research (November 2, 2026): Samsara dashboard guidance uses an overview and left navigation for fleet views and incident management (https://kb.samsara.com/hc/en-us/articles/48621492984589-Dashboard-Menus). Samsara telematics distinguishes a live feed from history and snapshots (https://developers.samsara.com/docs/telematics). project44 groups shipment visibility and exception handling in Movement (https://www.project44.com/platform/visibility/). FourKites describes temperature monitoring, threshold alerts and decision traces (https://www.fourkites.ai/outcomes/end-to-end-cold-chain-integrity). These inform information architecture and workflows; no copied UI, brand assets, customer metrics or capabilities are asserted.

The authorized browser simulator remains the data source, identified once in the environment bar and detailed in settings. No real shipment locations, drivers, GPS, contact notifications or backend approvals are fabricated. Filtering and exception counts derive from current generated records. Acknowledgement records local operator intent but never clears the condition or changes the recovery criteria; a changed condition resets acknowledgement. Notes are browser-local, bounded by 5000 characters per note, partitioned by seed/shipment; storage denial provides a download fallback. Exports explicitly retain training scope. Data persistence is not team synchronization.

Recorded source and engineering views are retained as evidence tools, not product-home marketing. Historical benchmark files and backend behavior are preserved. Obsolete landing/modal/illustration browser cases are replaced with portal-specific responsive, search, acknowledgement, local handover, safe text, export and storage-denial coverage; recorded-workspace, source, replay, planning and live-engine checks remain. Runner-only browser and visual review must qualify the exact source before publication under the owner release rule.
