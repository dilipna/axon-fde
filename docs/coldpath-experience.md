# Coldpath: make the project understandable before asking for technical attention

The user requested one final clarity and commercial-branding iteration after the verified AxonFDE decision-workspace release. Coldpath is the product name; the existing repository, deployment destination, AXON_ settings and historical source IDs remain unchanged.

## The change in experience

- Start with the problem in familiar language: medicines or food warming during delivery. Explain what an assistant helps organize before introducing orchestration or databases.
- A warm white/deep green visual system connects the overview, guided workspace and detailed engineering replay. Code-native route/truck/signal artwork is labeled illustrative rather than GPS or measured live output.
- The four-chapter guide explains the warning, conflicting rules, a proposal needing fresh approval, and the failed outcome. It is an editorial explanation of the saved record, not a new AI response or field success. Warning times come from the unchanged evidence.
- Technical logs, model output, source fingerprints and verifier cases are available in an explicit disclosure. Source links open the relevant original step. The original complete replay now lives at engineering.html and remains reachable.
- Browser planning is optional and clearly hypothetical. Short question answers lead with a plain-language explanation; the original quote and citations stay available in a disclosure. A readable brief leads with the plain-language story, then notes, availability assumptions and the technical record. The original evidence packet and source checks remain.
- The old evidence-versioned local note keys are retained, so the rename does not discard notes. Share links contain only the study and chapter. No account, extra backend, model call or new storage service is introduced.

## What must stay true

The original 35-minute single-incident warning is not a fleet average or proof of avoided loss. The compressor recording does not simulate post-action physics; its failed outcome and reopened incident remain visible. The sensor study contains no recorded intervention or approval. Frozen verifier tradeoffs remain paired. The public website cannot authorize or dispatch actions. Source hashes establish consistency, not authenticity.
